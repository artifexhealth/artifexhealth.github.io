Our mission.

One health app that brings your wearable, body, lab, and nutrition data together — then ties it all into insight you can actually use.

With backgrounds in professional gaming and a deep love for the health and wellness space, we design with intention. Seeing your own metrics is great — but we want you to do more than look. We want you to act. Artifex Health makes you aware of your metrics, gives you context behind them, and offers a personally curated path to improving them. Earn achievements, track your trends, and chat with an AI coach that pushes you toward better habits, every day.

Inside the app.

Artifex Health transforms raw wearable and phone data into meaningful feedback. Instead of disconnected metrics, it ties them together to help you understand what changed, why it matters, and what to do next.

  • Sleep stage & quality
  • Daily readiness
  • Heart rate trends
  • Activity tracking
  • Calorie burn
  • Nutrition logging
  • AI coaching
  • Personal goals

Key product highlights.

Nutrition intelligence

Log your food and get recommendations based on your current nutrient intake. Balance your day's profile while accounting for allergies, diets, and maintenance goals.

Sleep intelligence

Better context around your nightly sleep structure, duration, and recovery quality — so you understand what happened while you were sleeping.

Arti, your AI coach

Discuss your metrics with Arti — your personal AI with context into all your data. Correlate trends, ask about your blood work, or see how sleep changes with activity.

Readiness scoring

A concise daily signal that helps you decide whether to push, maintain, or recover — based on overnight recovery and prior exertion.
